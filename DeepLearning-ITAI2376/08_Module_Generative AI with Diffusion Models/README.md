Overview:

This module introduces diffusion models and explains how they generate realistic images by gradually
removing noise from random static. It covers important concepts such as forward diffusion, reverse 
diffusion, Gaussian noise, DDPM, DDIM, latent diffusion models, and classifier-free guidance. The 
presentation also explains the role of U-Net architectures, CLIP, and cross-attention in controlling 
image generation through text prompts. Overall, it provides a strong understanding of how modern AI 
systems like Stable Diffusion and DALL·E create high-quality images.

Diffusion Model Lab Notebook – Practical Implementation

The notebook focuses on the practical implementation of diffusion models and image generation 
techniques. It demonstrates how noise is added and removed step by step during the diffusion process
and includes experiments using U-Net architectures for denoising tasks. The notebook also explores how
model settings affect image quality, generation speed, and overall performance. Through hands-on 
experimentation, it develops practical skills in building and understanding modern generative AI 
systems.

Diffusion Model Reflection Report

The report reflects on the learning experience gained from studying and implementing diffusion models.
