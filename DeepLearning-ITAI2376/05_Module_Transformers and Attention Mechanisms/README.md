Overview:

This repository section focuses on Transformers and attention mechanisms, which power many modern AI
systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. The module explores how Transformers replaced traditional
RNNs by solving problems related to sequential processing and long-range memory. It introduces concepts
such as embeddings, positional encoding, self-attention, multi-head attention, BERT, GPT, and Vision 
Transformers. Through both theory and hands-on practice, this module builds a strong understanding of
the architecture behind modern generative AI systems.

This module explains the evolution from RNNs and LSTMs to Transformer-based architectures. It introduces
the attention mechanism and shows how Transformers process all words in parallel to better understand 
context and relationships between tokens. Topics such as embeddings, positional encoding, self-attention,
encoder-decoder architecture, BERT, GPT, and Vision Transformers are explained in a simple and practical
way. The presentation also discusses scaling laws and how Transformer models became the foundation of 
modern AI applications.

Lab – BERT & Vision Transformers

This lab focuses on applying Transformer concepts using real-world deep learning tools and frameworks. 
It includes fine-tuning pre-trained BERT models for sentiment analysis and experimenting with Vision 
Transformers (ViTs) for image-related tasks. The lab demonstrates how transfer learning allows powerful
pre-trained models to adapt quickly to specific tasks. It also provides practical experience with 
Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch, and attention visualization techniques.
