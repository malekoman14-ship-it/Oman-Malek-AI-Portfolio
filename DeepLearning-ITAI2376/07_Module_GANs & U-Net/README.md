Overview:

This module introduces Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and U-Net architectures, focusing on how
AI systems generate realistic images through adversarial learning. It explains the roles of the 
generator and discriminator, where two neural networks compete to improve image quality and realism.
The module also covers important concepts such as transposed convolutions, training instability, mode
collapse, CycleGAN, StyleGAN, and skip connections in U-Net. Additionally, it highlights how U-Net 
preserves fine image details and serves as the foundation for modern generative AI systems like Stable
Diffusion and DALL·E. Real-world applications including medical imaging, image generation, photo 
editing, super-resolution, and deepfake detection are also discussed throughout the presentation.
