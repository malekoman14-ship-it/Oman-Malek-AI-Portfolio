Overview:

This module introduces Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) and the foundations of generative AI, focusing
on how neural networks can create new content instead of only classifying existing data. It explains
the structure of autoencoders, including encoders, latent spaces, and decoders, and explores how VAEs
use probability distributions to generate realistic outputs. Important concepts such as reconstruction
loss, KL divergence, reparameterization trick, latent space organization, and transposed convolutions
are discussed in a simple and practical way. The module also highlights real-world applications of VAEs
in image generation, anomaly detection, data augmentation, drug discovery, and other modern AI systems.
